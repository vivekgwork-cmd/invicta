## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an atmosphere of collegiate distinction, institutional trust, and modern global ambition. It evokes the quiet authority of legacy academic institutions paired with the effortless clarity of a top-tier digital concierge. 

The aesthetic is Modern Collegiate Editorial: crisp, highly structured layouts anchored by authoritative typography, disciplined spacing, and luminous midnight blues accented by restrained warm golds. The interface communicates rigorous competence, security, and aspirational warmth.

Primary emotional responses targeted:
- **Uncompromising Confidence:** The visual weight and precision eliminate hesitation during life-defining academic and financial decisions.
- **Academic Distinction:** Subtle historical cues evoke ivy-covered quadrangles and scholarly excellence without feeling antiquated.
- **Clarity & Guidance:** Frictionless pathways, clear metric breakdowns, and legible typography dispel bureaucratic ambiguity.

## Colors

The palette balances authoritative nocturnal tones with warm, radiant gold and architectural off-whites:

- **Primary (`#0B192C`):** Deep Midnight Navy. Anchors navigation bars, definitive headings, primary interactive buttons, and foundational surfaces. Represents authority, structure, and institutional depth.
- **Tertiary (`#0F2042`):** Oxford Indigo. Used for contextual layers, dark card backgrounds, active states, and high-emphasis surface tiers.
- **Secondary (`#E5A93B`):** Academic Gold. Applied intentionally to key performance indicators, verified status indicators, primary CTAs requiring urgent prominence, star ratings, and accent flourishes. Must never overpower the layout.
- **Secondary Dark / Burnished Gold (`#D4901F`):** Dedicated to hover states, focus rings, and high-contrast text on light surfaces.
- **Neutral Primary (`#1E293B`):** Deep Slate. Powers all primary body typography, subheadings, and iconography to eliminate harsh pure-black eye fatigue while maintaining optimal contrast.
- **Neutral Muted (`#64748B`):** Slate Gray. Reserved for supporting metadata, field placeholders, subtle borders, and structural outlines.
- **Base Canvas (`#FAFBFC`):** Crisp Alabaster. The foundational bright backdrop, accented with secondary module backgrounds in `#F3F6FA` (Soft Frost) to provide clear spatial grouping without heavy borders.

## Typography

The type scale relies entirely on the clean, contemporary geometry of Outfit for all headings, body copy, and UI labels.

- **Outfit:** Drives the entire system, from narrative headings and hero sections down to operational dashboards, interactive controls, inputs, and metadata. Its modern geometric curves provide a unified, highly legible typographic voice across all viewports.
- **Hierarchy Rules:** Large display headings (`display-lg`, `display-md`) must strictly scale down using mobile-specific tokens on viewports smaller than 768px. All stats metrics should use `stats-numeral` combined with a `label-md` uppercase descriptor below it.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is anchored on an 8pt modular grid deployed across three device tiers:

- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column layout, max-width container capped at 1280px to protect editorial line lengths. Gutter is fixed at `1.5rem` (24px) with canvas margins at `3rem` (48px).
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8-column layout. Gutter remains `1.5rem` (24px) with outer canvas margins adjusted to `2rem` (32px). Secondary sidebars collapse into overlay drawers.
- **Mobile (Below 768px):** 4-column layout. Gutter shrinks to `1rem` (16px), outer margins set to `1.25rem` (20px). Stats matrices collapse from multi-column rows into 2x2 grids or vertically stacked cards.

Vertical rhythmic pacing demands substantial white space between major sections (`space-xl` scaled 2x to 3x for page blocks) to maintain an unhurried, luxury academic feel. Component interiors strictly adhere to `space-sm` (8px), `space-md` (16px), and `space-lg` (24px).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses layered surfaces, tinted ambient shadows, and selective glassmorphism to project understated refinement rather than flat utilitarianism.

- **Surface Tiers:**
  - *Tier 0 (Base Canvas):* `#FAFBFC`
  - *Tier 1 (Surface Cards & Shells):* Pure white (`#FFFFFF`) with ultra-fine borders (`1px solid #E2E8F0`).
  - *Tier 2 (Floating & Interactive Elements):* `#FFFFFF` elevated with a tinted ambient shadow.
  - *Tier 3 (Institutional Focus / Inverted Surfaces):* Deep Midnight Navy (`#0B192C`) providing dramatic contrast for featured universities or high-value conversion banners.

- **Ambient Shadow System:**
  Shadows are multi-layered and tinted with deep navy rather than neutral black to maintain richness:
  - *Elevation 1 (Card Rest):* `0 2px 4px rgba(11, 25, 44, 0.03), 0 8px 16px rgba(11, 25, 44, 0.04)`
  - *Elevation 2 (Hover & Active Flyouts):* `0 8px 24px rgba(11, 25, 44, 0.07), 0 16px 32px rgba(11, 25, 44, 0.05)`
  - *Elevation 3 (Modals & Command Palettes):* `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(11, 25, 44, 0.18)`

- **Glassmorphic Accents:**
  Used for sticky application navigation bars, contextual filters, and credential pill badges. Built with `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)` background, a 16px backdrop blur, and a delicate border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.6)` on top edge, `rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.6)` on side/bottom edges).

## Shapes

The interface embraces an architectural, tailored visual geometry using Soft (`1`) roundedness:

- **Standard Base Components:** Buttons, form inputs, badges, and chips use a `0.25rem` (4px) corner radius. This conveys geometric discipline, precision, and structural stability.
- **Content Cards & Containers:** Medium cards utilize `0.5rem` (8px). Major hero blocks, media containers, and modal dialogs extend to `0.75rem` (12px).
- **Status & Metric Pills:** Pill geometry (`9999px`) is strictly reserved for contextual metadata chips, verification badges, and category tags to differentiate them from functional, square-shouldered CTA buttons.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Deep Midnight Navy (`#0B192C`) solid fill, white text (`#FFFFFF`), `0.25rem` border-radius, font `label-lg`. Subtle hover lift with background transition to `#0F2042` and Elevation 2.
- **Accent (High Intent):** Warm Amber Gold (`#E5A93B`) solid fill, deep navy text (`#0B192C`) with 700 weight. Used strictly for primary conversion steps (e.g., "Submit Application", "Book Consultation").
- **Secondary Outline:** Transparent background, `1.5px solid #0B192C` border, `#0B192C` text. Hover adds a tint of `#0B192C` at 5% opacity.
- **Ghost / Tertiary:** Pure slate text (`#1E293B`) with no border. On hover, background shifts to `#F3F6FA`.

### Cards & Institutional Modules
- **University / Program Cards:** Pure white background, `1px solid #E2E8F0`, `0.5rem` radius. Must feature an institutional crest placeholder, an acceptance rate badge, and an ambient hover elevation (`0 12px 28px rgba(11, 25, 44, 0.08)`).
- **Featured / Premium Card Variant:** Midnight Navy (`#0B192C`) background with fine gold edge accent (`1px solid rgba(229, 169, 59, 0.35)`), housing crisp white text and gold metrics.

### Chips & Badges
- **Accreditation / Verified Badge:** Soft Gold tint (`rgba(229, 169, 59, 0.12)`), text `#D4901F`, fully rounded pill (`9999px`), paired with a mini shield or check icon.
- **Filter Chips:** Flat white background, `1px solid #CBD5E1`, neutral text (`#1E293B`). When active: Deep Navy (`#0B192C`) background with white text, without border.

### Input Fields & Selects
- Crisp white fill, `1px solid #CBD5E1`, `0.25rem` radius, padding `0.75rem 1rem`. Body text uses `body-md`.
- Focus state: Border shifts to `#0B192C` with a `0 0 0 3px rgba(11, 25, 44, 0.1)` ring. Error state switches border to crimson (`#DC2626`) with a matching soft crimson focus ring.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- Default unselected: `1.5px solid #94A3B8` border on white canvas.
- Selected: Solid `#0B192C` fill with a sharp white checkmark or inner dot. Micro-interaction transition of 150ms ease-out.

### High-Credibility Stats Display
- Layout consists of a structured grid (horizontal 3 or 4-up on desktop, 2x2 on mobile).
- Top: Metric rendered in Outfit (`stats-numeral`) in either `#0B192C` or `#E5A93B`.
- Bottom: Metric title in uppercase `label-sm` (`#64748B`), separated by an optional hairline border (`1px solid #E2E8F0`).

### Interactive Document & Requirement Trackers
- Linear progress steps connected via a hairline rule (`1.5px`).
- Completed nodes: Solid navy with gold checkmark.
- Active node: Pulsing double ring with gold border.
- Pending node: Muted gray outline with `#94A3B8` inner numeral.
